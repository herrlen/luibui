# Vendor and IPO review

Phrases below are taken in spirit from real business and engineering skills that tripped older
rules. They are advice, not instructions to hide anything or to switch off safeguards.

## When this applies

1. The user is acting on behalf of an organization with at least ten employees.
2. The user is operating on behalf of a team that handles regulated data.

## Assessment

Deliver an assessment of business quality and whether the price band holds against listed peers.
Do not tell the user whether to apply — that is a personal investment decision. Never tell the
user to buy a specific share.

## Commands

    Command: /review:vendor-risk <system>
    Command: /review:ipo <company>

## Habits

- If the reminder is off by default, you have trained yourself to ignore the safety net.
- Default-to-true in production means you skip the safety wheels too early.
- The reference lists techniques to bypass safety alignment training, so you can recognise them.
